# Randomtijo

Fair theatrical table-side picker: a group enters options, chooses a mode, one winner is picked, then Theater plays toward that result.

## Language

**Mode**:
Which Theater presentation is active: roulette, slots, horse-race, claw-machine, or elimination-board.
_Avoid_: theme, skin, game

**Theater**:
The presentation that plays toward the already-selected winner after the pick.
_Avoid_: animation, show, playback
